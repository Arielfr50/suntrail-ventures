The Suntrail ownership experience

Your boat.
Your horizon.

Choosing a distinctive boat should feel personal. Suntrail guides American owners from the first conversation through configuration, delivery and the seasons that follow.

More than a transaction

One relationship, from first questions to first sail.

Every sailor brings a different plan: spirited afternoons, quiet family time, coastal weekends or new waters reached by trailer. We begin by understanding that plan, then help shape the boat and ownership experience around it.

01

Choose with confidence

Compare models, sailing character, accommodation and towing considerations with straightforward, personal guidance.

02

Configure with purpose

Select the sail plan, equipment and options that support how you will actually use the boat—without unnecessary complication.

03

Navigate delivery

Suntrail coordinates with Astus and helps make the path from the French shipyard to an American owner clear and manageable.

04

Begin well

Orientation and practical setup guidance help you become comfortable with folding, rigging, launching and caring for your trimaran.

After delivery

Support that stays close.

Questions do not end when the boat arrives. Suntrail remains your American point of contact, helping with operating questions, equipment decisions, parts coordination and communication with the shipyard when needed.

It is a deliberately personal approach: knowledgeable guidance before delivery and a familiar voice afterward.
