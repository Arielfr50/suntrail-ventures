SUNTRAIL OFFLINE WEBSITE PREVIEW
================================

1. Extract the ZIP file on your Windows PC.
2. Open the extracted "Suntrail-Offline-Preview" folder.
3. Double-click "Suntrail-Preview.html".

The website will open in your usual browser. Scroll down to see every section.
The navigation buttons at the top also work.

Important: keep Suntrail-Preview.html, styles.css and the assets folder
together. The photograph will not appear if they are separated.

This preview does not change or publish www.suntrail-ventures.com.
